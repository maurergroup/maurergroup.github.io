Alexander Kanzow is an MSc student working at the intersection of electronic structure and machine learning with
a focus on improving and leveraging uncertainty quantification for reliable predictions.

## MSc Research

**Started:** November 2026

**Office:** 4.128, Institute of Physical Chemistry, University of Göttingen

Alex's MSc thesis deals with predictions of molecular Hamiltonians using equivariant graph neural networks. He will incorporate active learning based on uncertainty quantification for data-efficient and generalisable predictions.

## Educational Background

Alex completed his Chemistry Bachelor's degree at the University of Göttingen. In his second year, he participated in a research project led by Dan A. Obenchain, where he gained first experience in rotational spectroscopy. In his BSc thesis, he investigated hydrogen bonding properties of nitriles and isocyanides using vibrational spectroscopy under the supervision of Martin A. Suhm. In his MSc studies he specialised in computational chemistry and machine learning, including a research internship on machine learning, supervised by Giovanni Bistoni at the University of Perugia, Italy.
