Dr. Connor Box is a postdoctoral research fellow investigating how light interacts with chemical reactions on metallic surfaces to support the development of more efficient industrial processes powered by renewable energy.

## Research Focus

Connor's work addresses fundamental challenges in simulating metallic surfaces by examining electronic friction theory—a method that models coupling between nuclear motion and electronic degrees of freedom. His research aims to more rigorously define the limits of electronic friction theory and analyze these approximations.

This work is crucial for understanding photocatalytic processes, where light energy drives chemical reactions on metal surfaces, with applications in:

- Sustainable chemical synthesis
- Solar fuel production
- Green hydrogen generation
- Carbon dioxide reduction

## Computational Methods

By improving our understanding of electronic friction, Connor's research enables more accurate simulations of light-driven surface chemistry, helping to design better catalysts for renewable energy applications.
