The Faculty of Chemistry at the Georg-August-Universität Göttingen welcomes applications for a **Project Coordinator (f/m/d)** at the Theoretical Chemistry group (Group of Prof. Reinhard Maurer).

## Position Details

- **Location:** University of Göttingen, Germany
- **Duration:** until 28.02.2031 (full-time or part-time)
- **Start Date:** May 11, 2026 (or shortly after)
- **Salary:** Pay grade 13 TV-L (100%)
- **Contact:** Prof. Reinhard Maurer

## Research Focus

The Faculty of Chemistry at the Georg-August-Universität Göttingen is seeking to fill the following position starting May 11, 2026 (or as soon as possible): Project Coordinator (m/f/d).

The position is located at the Institute of Physical Chemistry in the Department of Theoretical Chemistry (Prof. Maurer). The role offers up to 100% of the regular weekly working hours (currently **39.8 hours/week**). The position involves the coordination of a 5-year large-scale research project as part of an **Alexander von Humboldt Professorship** and is limited until **February 28, 2031**. The position is generally available as a part-time role (at least 50%). Salary is paid according to **pay scale E13 TV-L**.

### Your Responsibilities
* **Central Coordination:** Manage a 5-year fixed-term research project in close coordination with international project partners across multiple locations.
* **Project Management:** Plan, monitor, and control project progress, milestones, and budgets in accordance with the requirements of the funding institution.
* **Communication:** Interface with funding agencies, partner institutions, and external stakeholders.
* **Reporting:** Prepare interim and final reports and document project results.
* **Events & Logistics:** Plan and execute international conferences, regular research group retreats, and cross-site research stays; provide logistical support for international guests and partners.
* **Data Management:** Develop, implement, and maintain a cross-site data management concept in close coordination with the IT departments of partner institutions; advise project members on research data management.
* **Recruitment:** Create and publish job advertisements for PhD and Postdoc positions in collaboration with project leaders; coordinate and administratively support the application and selection process.
* **Onboarding:** Onboard new employees and facilitate networking within the research group.
* **Third-party Funding:** Provide content-related and administrative support for the preparation and submission of grant proposals to national and international funding institutions (e.g., DFG, EU Horizon).
* **Strategy:** Research funding opportunities and support strategic planning for future applications.
* **Public Relations:** Actively design the project's external scientific communication (website, newsletter, social media).
* **Research Support:** If needed and given suitable prior experience, support research activities in the field of Theoretical Chemistry.

### Your Profile

#### Required:
* Completed university degree (Master’s or equivalent), preferably in natural science or related field; a PhD is an advantage.
* Excellent organisational skills and a structured, independent way of working.
* Business fluency in **German and English** (written and spoken), particularly proficient English skills.
* Willingness to undertake occasional business trips.
* Strong teamwork and communication skills for collaborating within a research institution.

#### Desired:
* Experience with administrative processes at German universities and/or research institutions.
* Knowledge of research data management and project management.
