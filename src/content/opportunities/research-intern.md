We offer summer research internships for undergraduate and master's students interested in AI research.

## Duration

10-12 weeks (June - August 2025)

## Locations

Positions available at all three lab locations (USA, UK, Germany)

## Projects

Interns will work on real research projects under the supervision of faculty and senior PhD students. Possible project areas include:

- Machine learning
- Natural language processing
- Computer vision
- AI ethics

## Eligibility

- Currently enrolled undergraduate or master's student
- Strong academic record
- Programming experience
- Passion for research

## Benefits

- Competitive stipend
- Housing assistance (if needed)
- Travel support
- Co-authorship opportunities

## How to Apply

Submit your application through our online portal by March 31, 2025.
