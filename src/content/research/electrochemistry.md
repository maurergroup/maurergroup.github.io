Electrochemical processes at solid-liquid interfaces underpin technologies for sustainable energy conversion, chemical synthesis, and sensing. Our research combines electronic structure theory, molecular simulation, and data-driven approaches to reveal how interfacial structure, solvent dynamics, and applied electrochemical potentials govern reactivity at the atomistic scale. By working closely with experimental collaborators, we connect atomistic simulations with advanced electrochemical and spectroscopic measurements to obtain a comprehensive understanding of interfacial phenomena.

## Current Projects

- Single-source precursors for the electrochemical growth of functional transition metal dichalcogenide semiconductors
- *Ab initio* simulation of electrochemical X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy
- Photo/electrocatalysis of oxygen evolution and methanol oxidation at zinc oxide surfaces
